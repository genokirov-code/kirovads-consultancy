2. Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following ways:

2.1 Audit Request Form

When you submit our free audit request form, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Company name
  • Industry / business type
  • Approximate monthly advertising budget
  • Advertising platforms currently used
  • Description of your main marketing challenge
  • How you heard about us
  • Consent confirmation (GDPR checkbox)

2.2 Analytics & Usage Data

We automatically collect anonymised and aggregated data about how visitors use our website, including pages visited, time on page, referral source, device type, and approximate geographic location. This is collected via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) through Google Tag Manager.

2.3 Advertising & Conversion Data

If you have interacted with our advertising on Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or Google, we may receive anonymised conversion signals to measure advertising performance. This data is processed in accordance with Meta's and Google's data policies.

2.4 Cookies & Browser Data

We use cookies and similar technologies. See Section 5 for full details.

2.5 Language Preference

We store your language preference (English or Bulgarian) in your browser's local storage under the key kirovads_lang. This is not a cookie and does not transmit data to any server.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR / EU GDPR Article 6:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Responding to audit requests, following up on enquiries, website analytics, and measuring advertising performance. We have conducted a legitimate interests assessment and are satisfied that our interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.
  • Pre-contractual steps (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to take steps prior to entering a service agreement.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Marketing cookies and advertising pixels, where we request your consent via our cookie banner.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): Where processing is required to comply with applicable law.

You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. To withdraw consent, contact us at geno@kirovads.com or clear your browser cookies and local storage.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Audit request form data: Retained for up to 2 years from the date of submission, or longer if a service agreement is entered into (in which case, standard accounting and contractual retention periods apply — typically 7 years).
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months (GA4 default retention setting).
  • Advertising conversion data: As per Meta and Google retention policies (typically 90 days for click-through data).
  • Email correspondence: Retained for the duration of the business relationship plus 2 years.

When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data where there is no compelling reason to continue processing.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at geno@kirovads.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.
